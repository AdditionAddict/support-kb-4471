How the split is made

  • The export pages overlap, so a report can come twice. Each id counts once.
  • A report’s problem comes from its tenant (locale and booking form, causes.ts) and the desk’s one-day notes, never from intake’s tag. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser, and the table shows how often that guess disagrees.
  • Merging on subject line (decision 0001) cannot tell them apart: the three problems arrive under the same subject lines.

Lessons

  • Same subject is not same cause. Before marking a big ticket known, group its reports by cause.
  • A footnote that says “read differently” is a problem nobody has filed yet.
