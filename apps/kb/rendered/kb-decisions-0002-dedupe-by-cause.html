Context

Decision 0001 merges reports whose subject lines match. #4471 shows what that costs: three problems arrive under the same subject lines, so all of them were merged into one ticket, marked known, and given one fix. The fix covered one of the three (#4472).

Decision

Two reports are the same issue when they have the same cause, not the same subject:

  1. look up the reporter’s tenant: its locale and its booking form (causes.ts, sameCause);
  2. never use intake’s tag: it is a guess from the reporter’s browser;
  3. a report the desk reads differently from its tenant’s cause (desk-notes.json) gets its own ticket, not a footnote under another one.

No cause on file: keep the report apart until someone reads it.

Consequences

  • Triage needs a tenant lookup instead of a glance at the subject. The lookup is code that already runs on every build.
  • A ticket’s count means one problem. A fix closes the ticket it fixes, not its neighbours.
  • 0001 stays as written; this record replaces it once accepted.
